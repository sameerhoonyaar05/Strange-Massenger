Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • DPDP Act (India) Compliant Notice

Your privacy is the absolute core of Strange Messenger. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how it is stored, and how your anonymity is rigorously protected.

1. Complete Anonymity Guarantee

When you use Strange Messenger:

  • Never Revealed: Your real Google name, profile picture, and email address are never displayed to other users or visitors.
  • What Others See: Only your chosen anonymous Nickname (e.g. "Neela Badal") and your selected State/UT postmark (e.g. "From Lucknow"). We never request or track your precise GPS coordinates.

2. Data We Collect & Why

  • Authentication Information: Your Google OAuth identifier and email address, processed securely via Supabase Auth for login verification and to prevent spam bots.
  • Profile Data: Your self-chosen nickname, selected state/UT, and 18+ age confirmation timestamp.
  • Letter Content: The text of letters and replies you choose to submit, their selected mood, and delivery timestamps.

3. How Your Data is Stored (Postgres RLS)

All data is hosted on Supabase's secure cloud infrastructure (PostgreSQL). We implement Row Level Security (RLS) policies on every single database table. This ensures that no user can query or inspect another user's private letters, email address, or identity.

4. Your Rights Under DPDP Act (India)

In accordance with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act):

  • Right to Withdraw Consent & Deletion: You may request the complete deletion of your account and associated letters at any time by contacting our grievance address.
  • No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Protection of Minors: In compliance with DPDP provisions concerning processing of children's data, this platform is strictly restricted to adults aged 18 and above.

For any privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, please contact the student maintainer at:
