Terms of Service

Last Updated: October 2026 • Student Non-Commercial Project

Welcome to Strange Messenger ("Letters to Strangers"). By accessing or using this website, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, please do not use the website.

1. Eligibility (18+ Only)

You must be at least 18 years of age to use Strange Messenger. By ticking the 18+ confirmation checkbox during onboarding, you legally affirm that you are of majority age under Indian law.

2. Community Safety & Writing Rules

Strange Messenger is designed as a calm, kind, and thoughtful platform for anonymous correspondence. You agree NOT to send any letter or reply that contains:

  • Personal contact information (phone numbers, email addresses, exact home/work locations, social media handles).
  • Web links, URLs, phishing attempts, or commercial promotions.
  • Abusive, harassing, defamatory, obscene, hateful, threatening, or sexually explicit content.
  • Promotion of self-harm, violence, hate speech, or any activity illegal under the laws of India.

3. Automated Moderation & Account Suspension

To keep the platform safe:

  • Any letter that receives 3 reports from distinct users is automatically hidden from all receivers.
  • If an account accumulates 3 hidden letters, sending privileges are automatically restricted for manual review.
  • We reserve the right to ban any account violating our community standards without prior notice.

4. Non-Commercial & "As-Is" Service

Strange Messenger is an educational, non-commercial student project developed in India. The service is provided on an "as-is" and "as-available" basis without any warranties of any kind. We are not liable for any content sent by anonymous users.
